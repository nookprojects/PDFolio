Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026

The short version: PDFolio converts files on your device. We don't upload your documents to any server. The only data we collect is your email address if you create an account.

1. Who we are

PDFolio is developed by Aperture Studio. Questions? privacy@pdfolio.app

2. What data we collect

Guest mode (no account): No personal data collected. A daily conversion count is stored locally on your device only.

With an account:

  • Your email address and hashed password
  • Files you explicitly choose to upload to cloud storage
  • File metadata (filename, size, date uploaded)

3. How your files are processed

All conversions — images to PDF, merge, split, compress — happen entirely on your device. Your documents are never sent to our servers. Files you upload to cloud storage are stored encrypted at rest via Supabase.

4. Third-party services

  • Supabase — cloud storage for Pro accounts. SOC 2 Type II compliant. Privacy Policy →
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. Does not receive payment details. Privacy Policy →
  • Apple App Store — all payments processed by Apple.

5. How we use your data

Only to provide the service. We do not sell data. We do not use data for advertising.

6. Data retention & deletion

Delete your account anytime from Settings in the app or by emailing privacy@pdfolio.app. All data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Access, correct, delete, or export your personal data at any time by contacting us.

8. Children

PDFolio is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Help Center

Support

Answers to common questions. Can't find what you need? Email us directly.

Getting started
How many free conversions do I get?
PDFolio is free with 3 conversions per day. The count resets every 24 hours. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited conversions.
Do I need an account to use PDFolio?
No. You can convert files without an account. An account is only needed for cloud storage to access files across devices.
What file types can I convert?
JPG, PNG, HEIC and other image formats to PDF. You can also save any web page as a PDF. PDF to image exports pages as JPG files.
Pro & subscriptions
How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → PDFolio → Cancel. You keep Pro access until the end of the billing period.
What's included in Pro?
Unlimited conversions, all PDF tools (merge, split, compress), cloud storage, and no ads — ever.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open PDFolio → Settings → Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
Does the Annual plan have a free trial?
Yes — 3-day free trial on the Annual plan. You won't be charged until the trial ends. Cancel anytime from iOS Settings.
Privacy & files
Are my files uploaded when I convert?
No. All conversions happen on your device. Files are never sent to our servers. Cloud storage only applies to files you explicitly upload with a Pro account.
How do I delete my account and data?
Settings in the app → Delete Account. All data permanently deleted within 30 days. Or email privacy@pdfolio.app.
Troubleshooting
The conversion failed — what do I do?
Try: (1) Open the file in another app to check it isn't corrupted. (2) Check available storage space. (3) Restart PDFolio. If the issue persists, email us with the file type and size.
My PDF is too large — how do I reduce it?
Use the Compress tool. It reduces file size by optimising the internal PDF structure.

Still need help?

We usually respond within one business day.
